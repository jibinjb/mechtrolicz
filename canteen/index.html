Return, Replacement & Refund Policy

100% Freshness & Satisfaction Guarantee
At EAT, UP!, our kitchen takes immense pride in hot, hygienic, flavorful meals. If an order fails our freshness or accuracy standards, you are 100% covered.
30-Minute Quality Window
If food arrives undercooked, cold, or with taste defects, submit a claim within 30 minutes of collection for an instant 100% wallet refund or free replacement.
Classroom Delivery Spills
Orders delivered to classrooms are packed with spill-resistant lids. If any container leaks or is delayed past lecture start, you are entitled to a full refund.
Strict Dietary Integrity
Strict segregation of vegetarian and non-vegetarian cookware. If an item is prepared incorrectly, we offer an immediate replacement plus full refund.
Instant Wallet Credit
Campus Wallet refunds reflect in your balance in under 10 seconds with zero deduction fees. Razorpay/UPI gateway refunds process in 2-4 hours.
How to Request a Return or Refund:
1
Go to Orders History or Live Tracker and locate your completed order.
2
Click Request Return / Refund and select your reason (Cold food, Wrong item, Spillage, Quality).
3
Choose Instant Campus Wallet Credit for immediate funds or Express Replacement Token to collect a fresh dish at Counter 1.
